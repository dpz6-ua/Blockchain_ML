\title[Integrating Blockchain and IPFS for Ex-Post Auditing in FL]{Integrating Blockchain and IPFS for Traceability, Integrity, and Ex-Post Auditing in Federated Learning}

\author*[1]{\fnm{David Piotr} \sur{Znojek}}\email{dawidznojek@gmail.com}

\author[1]{\fnm{Francisco A.} \sur{Pujol}}\email{fpujol@ua.es}

\affil*[1]{\orgdiv{Department of Computer Technology and Computation}, \orgname{University of Alicante}, \orgaddress{\street{Carretera de San Vicente del Raspeig s/n}, \city{San Vicente del Raspeig}, \postcode{03690}, \state{Alicante}, \country{Spain}}}

\abstract{Distributed training via Federated Learning (FL) preserves the privacy of sensitive edge data, but inherently lacks verifiable mechanisms to audit the authenticity and integrity of participant contributions, leaving systems vulnerable to model and data poisoning attacks with critical implications in domains such as Connected and Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs). This paper presents and evaluates a hybrid architecture that integrates a permissioned blockchain network based on Hyperledger Besu and IBFT 2.0 consensus with the Flower framework and off-chain content-addressable storage via IPFS. A Solidity smart contract implements a bidirectional cross-verification protocol between edge clients and the aggregation server using cryptographic commitments (IPFS content identifiers, CIDs) derived from neural network parameters. The viability of the framework is empirically validated on a physical distributed testbed training a convolutional classifier for vehicular hazard and road event detection under a rigorous multi-run protocol (15 complete independent executions totaling 450 federated rounds across five configurations). Quantitative results demonstrate that in a realistic distributed deployment, the blockchain auditability layer incurs a modest temporal overhead of only 9.5\% (6.34~s per round), while maintaining a deterministic, invariant EVM gas profile per transaction (186,749 gas initial cold write, 169,649 gas steady state) and scaling with marginal impact (+0.39~s in median round time and +0.10~s in block confirmation delay) when expanding the IBFT 2.0 quorum from 4 to 7 validator nodes ($f=1 \to f=2$). Furthermore, adversarial evaluation confirms deterministic interception of in-transit parameter tampering and unauthenticated injections at both client (149~ms) and server (31~ms) levels. An ex-post audit protocol allows independent entities to mathematically reconstruct the canonical FedAvg aggregation directly from immutable Besu and IPFS commitments in 0.34~s with exact numerical correspondence ($\Delta = 0$, CID match) and instantaneously detect deviations in 0.18~s. These findings confirm that the operational cost of decentralization, immutability, and non-repudiation is thoroughly viable for safety-critical distributed intelligent systems.}

\keywords{Federated Learning, Blockchain, Hyperledger Besu, IBFT 2.0 Consensus, IPFS, Ex-Post Auditing, Cryptographic Integrity, Traceability, Autonomous Vehicles}
